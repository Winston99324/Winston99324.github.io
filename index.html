I am a Ph.D. student in Informatics at UC Irvine. My research focuses on evaluating medical language and multimodal models and developing methods to extract clinical information, identify disease mentions, and detect errors in radiology reports.

My work examines the reliability of AI in clinical settings, from GPT-4V’s detection of findings on chest radiographs and the reasoning behind its medical predictions to how clinicians refine uncertainty in AI-generated documentation.

I also work on models and datasets for clinical NLP, including EvidenceOutcomes for extracting clinically meaningful outcomes from research literature, generative language models for detecting errors in radiology reports, and lightweight language models for disease detection.
